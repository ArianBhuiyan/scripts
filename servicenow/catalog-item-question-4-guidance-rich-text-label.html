Description:
Measures the impact to critical services should an asset in the class be unavailable or degraded and/or its data be unavailable or inaccurate.

Questions to consider:

  • Does this class support critical business services or applications that must remain resilient?
  • Are CIs in the class(es) actively used in services or operational workflows, such as Incident, Problem, Change, or eGRC?
  • If this class were not accurately inventoried or tracked, how would it affect day-to-day operations, such as Incident Resolution, Change Management, Patching, or Vulnerability Management?
  • Would loss or corruption of class data impede Business Continuity (BC) or Disaster Recovery (DR) capabilities?
  • Would inaccurate or missing records for this class delay service restoration after an outage or security event?
  • Would inaccurate or missing data for this class affect customer-facing applications or service reliability?
  • Are there automated alerts or remediation tied to this class?
