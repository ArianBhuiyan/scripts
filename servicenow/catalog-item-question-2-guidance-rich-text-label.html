Description:
This category assesses regulatory and compliance risk if the class or its data were inaccurate. It is used to assess the consequences of non-compliance with banking, state, or federal regulations, with a risk threshold established at one regulatory breach.

Questions to consider:

  • Has this asset class ever been highlighted by TRM, examiners, and/or auditors as an area requiring heightened scrutiny, remediation, and/or control enhancements?
  • If this class were unavailable, would it affect processes that feed into regulatory, risk, and/or financial reporting?
  • Have any changes occurred to your operating environment that increase the likelihood that inventory data could become outdated, incomplete, or otherwise inaccurate?
  • If so, have compensating controls been established to reduce the likelihood and impact of incomplete inventory data?
