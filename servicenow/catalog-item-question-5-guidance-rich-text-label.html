Description:
Evaluates cybersecurity and technical risks by examining the potential impact of threats, vulnerabilities, and patch management practices.

Questions to consider:

  • Have threat models, adversary techniques, and exploit paths for this asset class been assessed, documented, and addressed by controls?
  • Are there known vulnerabilities or attack vectors associated with it?
  • Does this asset class include systems or components that, if compromised, would provide administrative control or privileged access to other systems, applications, and/or network segments?
  • Is this class and its attributes scanned by vulnerability tools, such as Qualys or Aqua?
  • Would incomplete and/or inaccurate tracking of this class and its components or attributes result in blind spots that would reduce the effectiveness of vulnerability scanning, monitoring, and/or patching?
  • Would missing or stale CMDB data on these CIs delay patching or VRM response times?
