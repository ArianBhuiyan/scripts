Description:
This category quantifies and evaluates the impact on customers of service disruption, including how many customers are affected and the significance of the impact to them. It examines the potential for direct negative customer outcomes, marked by a threshold of 1,000 affected customers.

Questions to consider:

  • If assets in this class were unavailable or misconfigured, how many customers, internal or external, would likely be affected?
  • Would failure of a CI in this class cause significant disruption to operations or customer experience?
  • Is this CI class integrated with monitoring or observability tools?
  • How soon would service degradation to assets in this class be made visible? Are there automated alerts or remediation tied to this class?
  • Would inaccurate or missing data for this class affect service reliability?
